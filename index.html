Social Statistics

A website about the effects of social media and healthier digital habits.

Social platforms shape how people communicate, learn, and spend time online. This site explores those effects and suggests better ways to manage them.

Updated on April 15, 2026 for a first deployment presentation.

Why Social Media Matters

Social media influences how people think, interact, and spend their time. While it connects billions worldwide, it also affects mental health, attention span, and daily habits in powerful ways.

Global Reach

Over 4.8 billion people use social media worldwide, making it one of the most influential tools in modern life.

Daily Usage

The average person spends about 2–3 hours per day on social platforms.

Youth Engagement

Teenagers and young adults are the most active users, often checking apps multiple times per hour.

Information Source

Many people now get their news primarily from social media instead of traditional outlets.

Key Effects

Social media has both positive and negative impacts depending on how it is used.

Positive: Helps people stay connected across long distances and build communities.
Positive: Provides access to education, news, and diverse perspectives.
Negative: Can contribute to anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem.
Negative: Encourages excessive screen time and reduced real-world interaction.

Take Control of Your Habits

Understanding your usage is the first step toward healthier habits. Set goals, track your time, and build a better relationship with technology.
