Project facts

This page shares real statistics about social media use to help show how widespread it is and why healthy habits matter.

Global users: More than 5.79 billion people worldwide use social media each month.
Population share: Social media users now represent more than 2 in 3 people on Earth.
Annual growth: About 294 million new social media user identities were added over the past year.
Daily time: The typical user spends about 18 hours and 36 minutes per week on social and video feeds, which is roughly 2.5 hours per day.
U.S. usage: YouTube is used by about 83% of U.S. adults, and Facebook is used by about 68%.
Teen and young adult trend: Younger adults are more likely than older adults to use multiple social platforms regularly.

Why these numbers matter

Social media is now a major part of everyday communication, entertainment, and news consumption. These statistics show how important it is to use platforms intentionally instead of passively scrolling for long periods. They also support the idea that digital habits can have a real effect on focus, sleep, and productivity.
